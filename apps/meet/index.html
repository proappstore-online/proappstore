About

Meet is a zero-install video calling app. Toggle your availability, share a link, and get a push notification when someone joins your room. Video and audio are peer-to-peer via WebRTC — no media server, no latency, no data collection.

Designed for quick 1-on-1 calls, not large meetings. The availability toggle lets contacts see when you're free without messaging you first. Push notifications ensure you never miss a join — even if the tab is closed.

Meet is a Pro app on ProAppStore. It uses the ProAppStore SDK for authentication, real-time features, and data storage. The source code is available on GitHub.
