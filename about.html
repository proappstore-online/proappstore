About ProAppStore

A platform for premium web apps that work right in your browser. No downloads, no app store gatekeepers.

What is this?

ProAppStore is a collection of web apps that go beyond what a basic website can do. These apps have databases, file storage, real-time collaboration, maps, and payment processing built in. They run in your browser on any device, but they feel like native apps.

Think of it like an app store, but everything runs on the web. No downloads. No iOS vs Android. Just open the link and use it.

How does it work?

1. Browse and open

Find an app you like on this site. Click "Open" and it loads instantly in your browser. You can install it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

2. Sign in once

Use your GitHub account to sign in. One sign-in works across every app on the platform. Your data and settings follow you.

3. Use everywhere

Your data lives in the cloud. Start on your phone, continue on your laptop. Share with friends. Everything stays in sync.

What makes Pro apps different?

Our sister site FreeAppStore has 24+ free apps that store data locally on your device. Pro apps take it further:

  • Cloud data — Your information is saved in the cloud, not just on your phone. Switch devices and your stuff is there.
  • Real-time — Chat with others, collaborate on projects, see who's online. Apps can be social.
  • File uploads — Attach photos, documents, and videos. Apps can handle your files.
  • Maps — Find nearby events, plot locations, get directions. Location-aware apps without the complexity.
  • Payments — Developers can offer premium features. Users subscribe and manage billing in one place.

The Free + Pro family

FreeAppStore and ProAppStore are part of the same platform. Free apps are always 100% free forever — no tricks, no upsells. Pro apps add cloud features that cost real money to run (servers, databases, storage). The $9/month subscription covers those costs.

Same account works on both. You can use the free apps without ever paying. If you want cloud-powered apps, Pro is here.
