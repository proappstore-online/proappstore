No downloads, no app store. Open any app instantly on your phone, tablet, or computer. Install to your home screen for an app-like experience.

Sign in once with GitHub. Your account works across every app on the platform. Your data syncs, your settings follow you.

Your data lives in the cloud, not just on your device. Access it from anywhere. Share with others. Never lose your work.

Free and Pro — same family

ProAppStore is the premium sibling of FreeAppStore. Free apps are always 100% free with local storage. Pro apps add cloud databases, file uploads, real-time collaboration, maps, and more. Same account works on both.
