Frequently asked questions

What if I don't want to pay?

Every pro app has a free counterpart on FreeAppStore.online. The free version is always available — it's not a trial, it's a full app. Pro adds cloud sync, AI features, and collaboration on top.

How does billing work?

You subscribe to individual apps through ProAppStore. One payment method, one dashboard. App subscriptions are fixed monthly prices set by developers. Token costs (if you use AI features with your own keys) are billed separately based on actual usage.

Why do developers only pay 10%?

We believe infrastructure shouldn't eat most of a developer's revenue. Our platform handles billing, auth, and key management efficiently. 10% covers our costs and lets developers keep what they earn.

Is my API key safe?

Yes. Your keys are encrypted at rest and never exposed to app developers. Apps request AI completions through our proxy — they send prompts, we attach your key, make the API call, and return results. You can revoke access per-app at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any subscription instantly from your dashboard. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No lock-in — you can always fall back to the free version of any app.

What's Stripe Connect?

ProAppStore is the platform of record. Users subscribe through us, not through individual apps. This means one billing relationship for users and instant payouts for developers, all powered by Stripe Connect.
