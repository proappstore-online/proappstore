Help grow apps you believe in

Building an app is one way to contribute to ProAppStore. Helping the right people find it is another. Promoting and marketing are recognized contributions alongside coding, and you do not need to write code, own an app or have a GitHub account to take part.

Practical ways to help

  • Tell people who would use it. A friend, a colleague or a club that has the problem the app solves.
  • Publish something useful. A short demo, a walkthrough or a tutorial that shows the app doing real work.
  • Share where it is welcome. Post in relevant communities, with the moderators' permission and following their rules.
  • Bring it to events. Use QR materials at meetups or stalls where the app fits the audience.

Only share with people who would want to hear about it. ProAppStore does not send outreach for you and does not import your contacts.

How it works

  1. Choose an appPick one from the list below. You can promote several apps, each with its own link and totals.
  2. Sign in and enrollUse your ProAppStore account. Enrolling for an app takes a minute and gives you no developer or admin access.
  3. Get your personal link, code and QRThese identify you for that app only.
  4. Share itUse the activities above.
  5. Confirmed new users are attributed to youYou can see your totals and your contribution share in your promoter workspace.

Clicks and QR scans are not users. A referral counts only when a new person signs in, uses the app for the first time and the platform confirms it. Opening a link, scanning a code or downloading materials does not count by itself.

How recognition works

Once your first referral is confirmed, you are recorded as a Promotion / Marketing contributor to that app. This is project recognition, shown beside code contributions and never merged with them into a single score.

Your share of referred users for an app is your confirmed referred users divided by all promoters' confirmed referred users for the same app and the same period. It is not a share of all the app's users: people who find the app on their own are counted separately. If nobody has been referred in a period, the share is zero.

Public contributor profiles and team pages are still being rolled out. Your recognition is shown publicly only if you choose to make your profile public, and referred people are never named.

This is not a job and ProAppStore does not set income rates. Nothing on this page promises money or rewards for promoting an app.
