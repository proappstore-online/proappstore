The big picture

Where this is going

We're building a world where useful software doesn't need an app store gatekeeper or a 30% tax. Web apps are faster, safer, and work on every device. ProAppStore gives developers the tools to build real products — databases, payments, storage, collaboration — and gives users a single place to find and use them.

No walled garden. No platform lock-in. The web is the platform.

Priorities shift based on what people actually need. Have a suggestion? Tell us on GitHub.
