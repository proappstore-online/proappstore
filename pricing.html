Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay for ProAppStore?

No. ProAppStore is free during the trial. There is no subscription.

Will it stay free?

Apps may offer paid plans later. Nothing is charged today, and an app's price will be shown on its page before you pay.

Do I need an account for the free apps?

No. Free apps on FreeAppStore work without any account. Data is saved locally on your device.
