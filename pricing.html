For Developers

Publishing on ProAppStore is free. The platform provides hosting, databases, auth, payments, and storage at no upfront cost. The business model is simple:

$0 to start

No publishing fee, no monthly charge, no setup cost. Run pas create and start building.

10% commission

When users pay, the platform takes 10%. You keep 90%. Stripe handles processing (+ their standard 2.9% + 30c).

You set the price

Set your own subscription price. Monthly, yearly, or one-time. Your app, your pricing.

What Costs Us Money (and why we charge)

We're transparent about infrastructure costs. Here's what runs behind every Pro app:

ResourceProviderWhy it costs
D1 DatabaseCloudflarePer-app isolated SQLite. Reads, writes, storage.
R2 StorageCloudflareFile uploads. Charged per GB stored + egress.
WorkersCloudflareData workers, API backend. Per-request billing.
Durable ObjectsCloudflareWebSocket rooms. Charged per GB-second of duration.

StripeStripePayment processing. 2.9% + 30c per transaction.

The $9/month subscription and 10% commission fund these infrastructure costs and platform development. No VC money, no ads, no data selling.

Why Not Per-App Pricing?

We considered charging per-app but chose a single subscription instead. Here's why:

  • Lower friction — Users try more apps when there's no per-app purchase decision.
  • Better for small apps — A niche utility with 50 users gets the same infrastructure as a popular app.
  • Simpler for everyone — One subscription, one billing relationship, one dashboard.

Developers who want per-app pricing can set their own subscription prices through Stripe. The platform subscription is the baseline; per-app pricing is opt-in on top.
