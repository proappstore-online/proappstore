Frequently asked questions

What do I get for $9/month?

Access to every Pro app on the platform. One subscription, all apps. No per-app charges, ever.

Where does my $9 go?

10% covers platform infrastructure (hosting, databases, file storage, real-time servers, payments). The other 90% goes to the creators of the apps you actually used that month — split in proportion to how much you used each one. See the math.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data stays in your account. If you resubscribe later, everything is still there.

Do I need an account for the free apps?

No. Free apps on FreeAppStore work without any account. Data is saved locally on your device.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards via Stripe. We support all major card networks worldwide.

Is there a free trial?

Not yet, but we're considering it. The free apps on FreeAppStore give you a good feel for the platform.

Why does Pro cost money?

Running cloud infrastructure costs real money. Every Pro app gets its own database, file storage, and real-time servers. These cost us per-user, per-month. The $9 subscription covers those costs and funds platform development.

We don't run ads. We don't sell data. We don't have investors pushing for growth-at-all-costs. The subscription is the business model.

For developers — how you get paid

There are no per-app prices. Subscribers pay one $9/mo fee for the whole catalogue. At the end of each month, the subscription pool — minus the 10% platform fee — is divided among creators in proportion to how much their app was actually used.

Think of it like Spotify or Substack's "boost" model: a subscriber who spends 80% of their time in one app sends roughly 80% of their subscription fee to that app's creator. A subscriber who uses ten apps evenly splits theirs ten ways.

The math, made explicit

For each subscriber, in each month:

  1. The platform records how much that subscriber used each app (session-minutes plus API calls, normalised).
  2. The subscriber's $9 (minus the 10% platform fee) is split across the apps they used, proportional to that usage.
  3. At the end of the month, each creator's payout is the sum of those slices across every subscriber who used their app. Payouts land via Stripe Connect.

If your app gets 0% of any subscriber's time that month, you earn $0 that month. If your app is the only one a thousand subscribers use, you earn ~$8,100 that month (1,000 × $9 × 90%).

What this means for what you build

  • Don't gate features behind payments. Every feature is included with the $9 sub. There's nothing to upgrade to inside your app.
  • Don't show upgrade prompts. See above.
  • Build something people use. That's the entire incentive — earnings track usage, not screenshots.
